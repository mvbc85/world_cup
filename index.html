How It Works

  • Buy-in price: A$5 per participant.
  • Teams are assigned during a meeting on Thursday at 3:00 PM using a random selector.
  • Minimum viable pool is 16 participants, each receiving 3 teams.
  • After 16, additional participants are pending until the list reaches 24 participants (then 2 teams each).
  • After 24, additional participants are pending until the list reaches 48 participants (then 1 team each).
  • Use each confirmed participant's Assign Teams button to record meeting outcomes.
